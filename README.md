# BMW Sales Performance – Power BI

An interactive 3-page Power BI dashboard analyzing BMW vehicle sales data across regions, models, fuel types, and years. Designed to provide clear, data-driven insights for business decision-making.

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 – BMW Sales Performance
Snapshot of overall sales metrics for 2024:
- **Sales Volume**: 18M units sold
- **Total Revenue**: $1.31 Trillion
- **Distinct Models**: 11
- Sales breakdown by model (bar chart)
- Revenue distribution by fuel type (pie chart)
- Average price per model (table)
- Revenue by region (world map)

### Page 2 – Year Wise Performance
Trends and patterns across years (2010–2024):
- Sum of Revenue by Year (line chart)
- Sum of Sales by Year (line chart)
- Average Price (USD) by Year (line chart)
- Sales by Transmission type (Automatic vs Manual) per year
- Sales by Classification (High vs Low) per year
- Filters: Model and Fuel Type slicers

### Page 3 – Performance Drivers
Deep-dive into factors influencing sales and pricing:
- Sum of Sales by Region (dot plot)
- Average Mileage by Year (line chart)
- Sum of Sales by Color (bar chart)
- Average Engine Size by Year (table)
- **Key Influencers Visual**: Identifies factors that drive Average Price (USD) increases

## How to View

1. Download the `.pbix` file
2. Open with [Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/desktop/) (free)
3. Explore all 3 pages using the navigation buttons
